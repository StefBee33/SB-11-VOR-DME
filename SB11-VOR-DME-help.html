Description:

Calculate an aircraft's current geographical latitude and longitude (sometimes also called a fix) from the known position of a VOR/DME station, the station's magnetic declination, the aircraft's magnetic radial FROM that station, and its DME distance. Optionally, enter the aircraft's height above the station to correct the DME slant range to horizontal distance.

Operating conditions:

  • The calculator must use DEG mode, not RAD or GRAD. The initialization functions D, D' and E' enforce DEG mode.
  • Southern latitudes and western longitudes are negative.
  • Magnetic declination is positive east and negative west.
  • Distances are entered in nautical miles and height differences in feet.
  • Enter the magnetic radial FROM the station, not merely the OBS course selected on the receiver (unless the CDI is centered with a FROM indication).
  • Before first use, press 2nd E' to initialize the program.

Usage:

To initialize for a new VOR/DME station, press 2nd E'. Enter the station data, followed by the aircraft's radial, DME distance and optionally height:

Press E to calculate. The aircraft latitude appears on the display; its longitude is placed in the test register t. After calculation, A', B', C', A, B and C retrieve their stored values instead of accepting new ones.

To change the radial, DME or height without clearing their current values, press D; then enter only the values you want to change. To clear all three position inputs, press 2nd D'. To change to a different VOR/DME station and clear all station and position inputs, press 2nd E'.

Function keys:

  • 2nd A' — enter or retrieve vor/dme station latitude.
  • 2nd B' — enter or retrieve vor/dme station longitude.
  • 2nd C' — enter or retrieve station magnetic declination.
  • A — enter or retrieve magnetic radial from the vor.
  • B — enter or retrieve dme slant range.
  • C — enter or retrieve height above the vor/dme station (optional).
  • D — Switch A, B and C to input mode without changing their stored values.
  • 2nd D' — Clear radial, DME and height; switch A, B and C to input mode.
  • 2nd E' — Clear all station and position inputs; switch all six input/output keys to input mode.
  • E — Calculate aircraft position; switch all six input/output keys to output mode.

Output:

  • Display (x): aircraft latitude, DDDMM.mmmmm.
  • Test register t: aircraft longitude, DDDMM.mmmmm.
  • Register 07: aircraft latitude, DDD.ddddddd.
  • Register 08: aircraft longitude, DDD.ddddddd.
  • Register 09: corrected true radial, DDD.ddddddd.

Coordinate conversion functions:

SBR . converts DDDMM.mmmmm to decimal degrees DDD.ddddddd. SBR +/- converts decimal degrees DDD.ddddddd to cockpit format DDDMM.mmmmm. Both return the result in the display and register 00.

Good to know:

The program does not interrupt ongoing calculations. Its user-defined functions do not use CLR or = internally, so stored arithmetic operations are not prematurely cleared or evaluated.

The program uses a planar approximation to calculate latitude and longitude differences. If the optional height is supplied, it first converts the DME slant range to horizontal distance.
